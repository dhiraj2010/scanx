How to write a scan

Write one condition per line. A stock shows up only when every line is true on the latest bar. Lines starting with # are notes.

Prices: open high low close volume

Averages: sma(close, 50) ema(close, 21) avg_volume(20)

Highs and lows: highest(high, 20) lowest(low, 10) high52 low52

Earlier bars: ago(close, 1) is the previous bar; ago(sma(close, 40), 4) is the 40-bar average 4 bars ago.

Momentum: change(1) (% change over 1 bar), rsi(14), macd(), macd_signal(), rs (RS rating 0–99 against the chosen list)

Others: atr(14) bb_upper(20, 2) bb_lower(20, 2) gmma_short gmma_long

Crosses: crossed_above(ema(close, 9), ema(close, 21)), crossed_below(close, sma(close, 50))

Joining: and, or, not, brackets, and ranges like 40 < rsi(14) < 60.

Examples
close > 1.05 * ago(close, 5) up 5% in 5 bars
(high52 - close) / high52 < 0.05 within 5% of the 52-week high
volume > 2 * avg_volume(20) and change(1) > 2

Bars are daily or weekly depending on the switch at the top. Weekly scans use the last completed week.
